Essential Barista Knowledge

Coffee Knowledge Hub

Great coffee starts with understanding your grind, brew techniques, and bean storage. Learn how to get the finest flavor from every cup.

1. The Grind Size Matrix

Grind size controls how fast water extracts flavor from coffee. Matching the grind to your brewing tool is the #1 secret to a great cup:

  • Extra Fine (Powder): Essential for Rakweh / Turkish coffee. Extracts instantly under heat.
  • Fine: Best for Espresso machines and Moka Pot.
  • Medium: Ideal for V60, Chemex, and classic drip filter coffee.
  • Coarse: Perfect for French Press and Cold Brew to prevent bitterness.

2. Perfect Rakweh Ritual

Brewing authentic Middle Eastern Rakweh coffee requires patience, precise water-to-coffee ratio, and heat control:

  1. Water First: Measure cold water in your Rakweh pot (approx. 60ml per cup).
  2. Add Coffee: Add 1 heaped tablespoon of fine Rakweh coffee per cup (add sugar now if desired).
  3. Gentle Stir: Stir well cold, then place over low heat. Do not stir again.
  4. Watch the Foam: As it comes to a simmer, a rich golden foam (face/ وجه) will rise. Remove from heat just before boiling!

3. Freshness & Degassing

Keep your specialty coffee tasting as fresh as the day it was roasted by protecting it from its 4 enemies:

Air, Moisture, Light & Heat

Always store your beans in an airtight container with a one-way degassing valve in a cool, dark cupboard.

Never Fridge Your Beans!

Storing coffee in the refrigerator creates condensation that destroys delicate aromatic oils.
